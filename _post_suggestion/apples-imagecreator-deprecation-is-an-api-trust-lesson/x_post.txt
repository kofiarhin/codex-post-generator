The Apple ImageCreator deprecation is an API trust lesson with a compile error attached.

On iOS 27 and Apple's other new OS releases, beta and TestFlight builds using ImageCreator will hit a runtime error. Public releases will stop compiling.

Apple's answer: show the system-managed Image Playground sheet or move to another service.

That is not a rename. It changes control, privacy, cost, and product behavior.

Platform AI can be useful. Just do not mistake "available in the SDK" for "stable infrastructure." Design the exit before the vendor designs it for you.
