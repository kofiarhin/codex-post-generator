The node-ipc supply chain attack is a reminder that expired domains are now package manager infrastructure.

On May 14, 2026, malicious `node-ipc` versions 9.1.6, 9.2.3, and 12.0.1 were published. Reporting tied it to an expired maintainer email domain / dormant account takeover.

That is the uncomfortable bit.

This was not exotic wizardry. It was identity lifecycle debt with a publish button.

If an old email domain can help resurrect trust in a dormant maintainer account, your dependency graph has a time machine pointed at your CI secrets.
