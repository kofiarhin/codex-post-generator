Kubernetes vulnerability scanning may get louder on June 1. Your cluster did not suddenly become less secure. The data got more honest.

Several old Kubernetes CVE records incorrectly implied fixed versions existed. The project is correcting them, so scanners may surface architectural risks that need configuration mitigations: an RBAC audit, control-plane DNS consistency, and webhook hardening.

The annoying lesson: not every security alert ends with a version bump. Sometimes the patch is ownership.
