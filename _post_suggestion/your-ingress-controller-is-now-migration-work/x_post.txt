Ingress NGINX migration is not a YAML cleanup task.

The controller may be retired, but your cluster still has years of annotations, rewrites, TLS assumptions, and routing behavior quietly holding production together.

Moving to Kubernetes Gateway API or another maintained controller starts with inventory, testing, and admitting that "installed years ago" is not documentation.

Infrastructure debt is patient. It waits until a critical dependency retires, then schedules the archaeology work for you.
