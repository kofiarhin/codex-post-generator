# Anti-Slop Review

Before returning a LinkedIn post, remove:
- Generic hype, corporate phrasing, and exaggerated claims.
- Fake controversy, suspense, invented anecdotes, and engagement bait.
- Empty claims such as 'game changer', 'the future is here', or 'this changes everything'.
- Repeated one-line slogans and robotic 'not X, but Y' patterns.
- News summaries without a clear engineering judgment.
- Unexplained technical claims or recommendations without trade-offs.
- Repeated agent-safety angles without a genuinely new mechanism.
- Private project names, architecture, issue IDs, URLs, metrics, or unreleased plans.
- Unsupported 'I built', 'I tested', 'we deployed', and performance claims.

Review for: one thesis, a specific mechanism, evidence, limitations, natural paragraph rhythm, useful reader takeaway, and a defensible headline.

Do not sacrifice technical accuracy just to make a post punchier. If a claim cannot be verified, qualify or remove it.

Reject posts that only describe a problem without a mechanism or useful next step. Check recent posts for repeated openings, arguments, and conclusions.
